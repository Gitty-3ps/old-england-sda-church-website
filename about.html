About Our Church

A community rooted in faith, family, and the gospel of Jesus Christ

Who We Are

Kool Name Seventh-day Adventist Church is a vibrant community of believers located in the beautiful hills of Manchester, Jamaica. We are dedicated to sharing the teachings of Jesus, worshiping together, and serving the wider community around us.

Every Sabbath, we open our doors to anyone seeking connection, worship, and a deeper walk with God — whether you're a lifelong member or visiting for the very first time.

Our Mission

To proclaim the everlasting gospel in the context of the three angels' messages of Revelation 14:6-12, leading people to accept Jesus as their personal Savior and unite with His remnant church, discipling them to serve Him as Lord, and preparing them for His soon return.

What We Believe

As Seventh-day Adventists, we believe in the Bible as God's inspired Word, salvation through Jesus Christ, the seventh-day Sabbath as a day of rest and worship, and the soon return of Jesus. Learn more about our fundamental beliefs on the General Conference website.
